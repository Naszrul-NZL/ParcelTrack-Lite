# Week 5 interface design

Direction approved by the team on 21 October 2026: courier operations, navy, white and orange, with clear parcel tables. This is the checkpoint prototype's direction, not an adopted RapidExpress logo or corporate identity.

Use navy #152e48 for navigation and primary actions, white #ffffff for work surfaces, paper grey #f3f5f7 for the workspace, dark text #172b3a, secondary text #526171 and orange #a94110 for the current task. These pairings provide readable text without a decorative gradient. Status names always accompany colour.

Use Segoe UI for familiar Windows/browser form reading, with a system sans-serif fallback. Align content left. A compact desktop navigation rail opens into a parcel work table; forms and parcel detail replace the work area. On phones, navigation becomes a horizontal wrapping strip, forms stack, and parcel rows reflow.

Energy 2, rhythm 2, motion 1. A shipping-label style tracking identifier is the distinctive detail; it identifies the parcel, not a decoration. No generated logo, avatars, stock illustrations or background texture. Use borders to separate actual records, not to frame every sentence. Buttons have 6px corners, work surfaces 10px, status labels 4px. No decorative animation or shadows. Persistent prototype disclosure explains the real data/security boundary.
